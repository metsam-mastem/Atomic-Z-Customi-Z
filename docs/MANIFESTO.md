# Atomic-Z-Customi-Z — Manifesto & Philosophy

## Manifesto
We believe customization is a form of authorship. A motorcycle is not only a machine; it is a canvas for identity, craft, and technical curiosity. Atomic-Z-Customi-Z aims to make that creative process visible and shareable through a clear, responsive, and extensible digital garage.

We build with precision in the interface, humility in engineering claims, and openness in our architecture. A beautiful render is a proposal—not a certified design. A saved configuration is a reproducible artifact—not proof of physical compatibility.

## Philosophy
- **Design is interaction:** meaningful controls should visibly affect the chosen object.
- **Visual truth:** concept geometry must never be presented as factory CAD.
- **Composable creativity:** colors, segments, parts, and effects should be modular.
- **Reproducibility:** each build should serialize, validate, migrate, and round-trip.
- **Open development:** architecture decisions, constraints, tests, and limitations are documented.
- **Craft and restraint:** effects support form; they do not obscure the motorcycle.
- **Progress by evidence:** prototype, verify, benchmark, then promote to stable.

## Product promise
Create, inspect, save, and present visual motorcycle customization concepts. Hardware export and engineering-grade fitment remain conditional on calibration and independent validation.

## Motto
**Ride Your Style. Light Up Your Ride.**
